## Method

Title: Seven operations. Then stop.

Lead: These are the only operations the method runs.

1. Input — What is present, before it is explained?
2. Decomposition — Event, behavior, interpretation, emotion, judgment, assumption, context, identity, unknown.
3. Contextual comparison — Where has something like this appeared, and what was different?
4. Pattern detection — What repeats, and under what conditions?
5. Contradiction detection — What does not agree with itself?
6. Evidentiary separation — Evidenced, inferred, symbolic, unknown.
7. Observational report — What can be seen without deciding what to become or do?

Then stop.

A question layer exists only after interest is shown. Questions come from the material just given. They do not choose a path.

## For practitioners

Title: For the person whose work is other people’s material

Lead: PCI can be a development tool for a practitioner. It is not a delivery system for clients.

Body: A coach, teacher, or operator may put their own residue through the seven operations: a pull to rescue, a certainty that arrived early, a week organized around someone else’s fragment. The report ends at observation. It does not say what to say next.

It is not supervision. It is not client homework. It does not score empathy.

The occupational test is the same as the article: has the practice kept a center of gravity, or has it evolved around fragments taken from the people who pay?
