# Design

## Palette

- Paper: #F7F3EA
- Ink: #2A2723
- Muted: #6B6558
- Gold: #8A7344
- Rule: #C8C2B4
- Dark field, if needed: #1C1916

Gold is a rule and a kicker, not a fill. Do not make buttons gold-on-gold.

## Type

- Titles: EB Garamond, or another old-style serif. Not a display script.
- Labels and nav: a plain grotesque, small, tracked slightly open.
- Body: serif, 18–20px, generous line height.
- No all-caps paragraphs. Kickers may be small caps.

## Layout

- Wide margins. One column for reading.
- Chapter pages feel like a book, not a landing page.
- No hero video of a person smiling at a laptop.
- No testimonial carousel unless a real sentence is supplied later. Do not invent quotes.
- No progress bars, badges, levels, or “day 4 of 30.”

## Image law

Stone, ivory, aged paper, a single object: a scale, a seed, an empty stage, a lens facing a lens. Slow. No wellness gradient. No hands in prayer. No brain diagrams.

## Motion

Almost none. A rule drawing in is enough. Do not animate type word by word.

## Components to build

- Chapter card: number, title, one line, link
- Article card: letter, title, one line
- Operation row: number, name, question
- Boundary list
- Footer lockup

## Components not to build

- Quiz
- Type result
- Mood ring
- Streak
- “Your practice for tonight”
- AI chat that advises
- Pricing table that promises transformation
