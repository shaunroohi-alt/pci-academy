# PCI website handoff

Give this folder to the website builder as the only source of truth. Do not invent chapters, types, practices, or calls to action that are not in this brief.

## What to build

A public site for Psycho-Creative Intelligence (PCI) and the book *The Art of Being*.

The site is a library and an entry. It is not a coaching app, not a course platform, and not a wellness product. If the builder can only make pages, build the public site. Do not fake a diagnostic, a type result, a streak, or a “what to do tonight” flow.

If the builder can also make a member area, the only allowed tools are:

- Observe — paste material, run seven operations, report, stop
- Journal — private write and archive; analysis is optional and uses the same loop
- On the Contrary — scenario in, configuration out, reflect, stop
- Library — the twelve chapters and five articles

Questions appear only after the member explicitly asks for them. They are generated from the material just given plus the live corpus. They do not assign a direction.

## One sentence

PCI makes structure visible, then stops. Direction stays with the person.

## Voice

Observational. Direct. No pep talk. No “you should.” No “tonight.” No types. No healing promise. No streak. Short sentences are allowed. The closing line of the system is: PCI observes. PCI reports. Then PCI stops.

Footer on every page: Education, not therapy. Not medical, clinical, or crisis care.

## Live content only

- Seven operations
- Twelve chapters of *The Art of Being* (Author’s Voice rewrite)
- Five companion articles, including YOUR “BUSINESS” EVOLVES AROUND OTHERS
- Sub-chapters under Individualism: Other People’s Material; Coherence in Business

Retired. Do not publish as current:

- Sixteen-chapter edition as the reading order
- Twelve Academy teaching principles as the method
- Booklets
- Reading types (Becomer, Resister, Doer, Rehearser, Unready, Unmet) as identity labels
- Guided practices attached to a result
