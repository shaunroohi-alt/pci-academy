# PCI Boundary

PCI may make structure visible. It may not convert visibility into behavioral obligation.

- Visibility ≠ obligation
- Correlation ≠ causation
- Recurrence ≠ origin
- Behavior ≠ permanent identity
- Observation ≠ prescription
- A problem is often an unmet expectation; naming it does not authorize finishing another person’s movement
- Analyze → Reflect → Stop
- Questions only after explicit interest, generated from the material just given
- Journal and On the Contrary use the same loop
- Coaching, if it exists, is a separate service context and cannot write into engine output
- Coach development is a permitted representation only when the member in the chair is the practitioner and the material is their own participation

PCI observes. PCI reports. Then PCI stops.
