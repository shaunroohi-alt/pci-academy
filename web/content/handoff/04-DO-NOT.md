# Do not

The builder must not add any of the following, even if a template suggests it.

- A thirteenth to sixteenth chapter as current text
- The twelve Academy principles as the method of the site
- Booklets as products
- Reading types as results: Becomer, Resister, Doer, Rehearser, Unready, Unmet
- “Discover your type”
- Guided practices, challenges, or 30-day programs
- Streaks, points, levels, compliance
- “What you should do tonight”
- Automatic follow-up questions
- Client-facing coaching homework
- “AI supervision”
- Healing, trauma-processing, or clinical claims
- Income or career promises
- Fake testimonials
- Stock photography of meditation, sunrise yoga, or smiling founders
- A chatbot that completes the user’s life

If a template section does not fit this list, delete the section.

## Allowed ending

PCI observes. PCI reports. Then PCI stops.

## Interest rule

If a member area exists, questions stay off until the person asks. Example of an allowed request: “Ask me questions from this material.” The questions must come from what they wrote and from the live chapters. They must not end in an assigned action.
